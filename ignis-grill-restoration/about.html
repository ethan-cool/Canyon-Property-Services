Our story

We started with one simple idea

Most people love their grill and hate cleaning it. A truly deep clean is messy, takes hours, and means taking apart parts you might not know how to put back. So most grills just never get one.

We started Ignis to fix that. We bring the tools, do the grimy work at your place, and hand you back a grill that heats evenly and cooks clean. Ignis is Latin for fire, and keeping that fire clean and safe is the whole job.

We’re based in [city] and serve [service area]. When you call, you’re talking to the people who’ll actually show up.

How we work

What you can count on

Detail-focused

We take the grill apart and clean the parts nobody sees. Burner ports, the underside of flavorizer bars, the corners of the firebox. That’s where the difference is.

Respectful of your home

We put down drop cloths, contain the mess, and treat your patio, deck or kitchen like it’s our own. When we leave, the area is clean.

Respectful of your time

We show up when we say we will, give you a clear price up front, and let you know if anything changes. No upselling, no runaround.

Straight answers

If a part is worn out, we’ll tell you. If it still has years left, we’ll tell you that too.

Locally owned

We live here, grill here and work here. Your business supports a local company, not a franchise.

Safety first

We check for the grease buildup and blocked ports that cause flare-ups, and point out anything that looks unsafe.

Standard Deep Clean

Our core service and the right pick for most home grills. We take it apart, clean every component inside and out, and put it back together. Ideal once or twice a year.

  • Gas, charcoal & pellet
  • Done in your driveway or patio

What’s included

  • Full disassembly of grates, flavorizer bars, heat shields and burners
  • Grates degreased and scrubbed
  • Burners cleaned and clogged ports cleared
  • Firebox scraped of carbon and grease
  • Drip tray and grease cup cleaned

  • Light inspection with notes on any worn parts
  • Work area cleaned up before we leave

Full Restoration

  • Heavy buildup & rust
  • Part replacement available

Everything in the Deep Clean, plus

  • Heavy carbon and rust removal on grates and internals
  • Detailed exterior restoration and stainless polishing

The appointment

How long does a cleaning take?

A Standard Deep Clean on a typical home gas grill usually takes about 1.5 to 2.5 hours. Larger grills, built-ins and Full Restorations can take 3 to 4 hours or more. We’ll give you a time estimate with your quote.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. If we can get to the grill (a side yard, patio or driveway) and you’ve given us the go-ahead, you don’t need to be there. We’ll send you before and after photos when we’re done.

For your first visit, we like to say hello if you’re around, so we can hear about any issues you’ve noticed.

What do I need to do to get ready?
  • Make sure the grill is cool and turned off.
  • Let us know if there’s a gate code, dog or tricky access.
  • If possible, point us to an outdoor water spigot.

That’s it. We bring everything else.

Will it make a mess?

No. We lay down drop cloths, contain the grease and debris, and take the waste with us. Your patio or deck is left clean.

Grills & cleaning

Which types of grills do you service?

Gas (propane and natural gas), charcoal, and pellet grills, from all the common brands. We also service built-in and outdoor kitchen grills, plus commercial grills for restaurants, breweries and HOAs. If you have something unusual, send us a photo and we’ll let you know.

How often should a grill be professionally cleaned?

For most households, once a year is the minimum, ideally in spring before grilling season. If you grill more than once or twice a week, twice a year (spring and fall) keeps grease buildup and flare-ups in check.

Restaurants, breweries and shared HOA grills usually need service every month to every quarter, depending on use. Our maintenance plans make this automatic.

What products do you use? Are they food-safe?

We use degreasers made for grills and cooking equipment, and rinse every food-contact surface thoroughly before putting the grill back together. Your grill is ready to cook on when we leave.

Can you fix or replace parts?

We do a light inspection with every cleaning and will point out worn grates, burners, flavorizer bars or igniters. With a Full Restoration, we can source and install replacement parts. We always quote parts first and never install anything without your OK.

What we do

A real deep clean, not a quick scrape

A wire brush cleans the top of the grates. The grease, carbon and rust that cause flare-ups, uneven heat and off flavors live underneath. We take the grill apart and clean all of it.

While we’re in there, we’ll do a light inspection and tell you straight if any parts are worn and worth replacing. No pressure, just a heads-up.

Every cleaning covers

  • Grates degreased and scrubbed
  • Burners cleaned and clogged ports cleared
  • Flavorizer bars and heat shields de-carboned
  • Firebox scraped and degreased
  • Drip tray and grease cup emptied and cleaned
  • Exterior cleaned and polished
  • Work area left clean

Why it matters

A clean grill cooks better and lasts longer

It isn’t just about looks. Here’s what a proper cleaning does for you.

Safer food

We strip out built-up grease, carbon, rust and old food residue. That gunk can harbor bacteria and flake onto whatever you’re cooking.

Lower fire risk

Grease buildup is a leading cause of flare-ups and grill fires. Clearing it out is the simplest way to cook with peace of mind.

Better performance

Clean burners and clear ports mean even heat, faster preheating and fewer hot and cold spots across the grates.

Better-tasting food

No more burnt-on residue or rancid grease flavors. Your food tastes like your food.

Longer grill life

Grease and rust eat away at metal parts. Regular cleaning protects your investment and pushes off the day you need a new grill.

Zero hassle

We come to you, do the dirty work on-site, and leave your patio or kitchen area as clean as we found it.

FAQ

Good questions

When should I schedule my cleanup?

Most Front Range trees drop their leaves from early October to mid-November. Big cottonwoods and oaks can hang on longer. If you have a lot of trees, two visits (one mid-season, one after the last leaves fall) usually works best.

How much does it cost?

It depends on yard size, how many leaves there are, and whether you want haul-away. We’ll confirm a price with you before any work starts, and booking online doesn’t commit you to anything.

Do I need to be home?

No. Just make sure we can get into the yard (leave a gate code in the notes if needed) and keep pets inside while we work.

What if it snows on my day?

Colorado fall weather is unpredictable. If snow or high winds roll in, we’ll reach out to reschedule for the next good day.

What happens to the leaves?

We can bag and haul them away, or mulch them into your lawn as free fertilizer. Just tell us which you prefer in the notes.
